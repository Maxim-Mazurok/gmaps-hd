Google Maps HD Downloader

Photos Click "Download HD" to save the current photo at full original resolution.
Videos Click "Download Video" and choose quality (best available, 1080p, 720p, 360p).
Bulk download Open a place’s Photos gallery, then click "Download all photos". The gallery scrolls to load more items before saving unique photos to Downloads/gmaps-hd/. Videos are skipped unless "Include videos" is checked. Use Stop to cancel.

Google may limit the gallery. The status reports when no more photos load; it cannot guarantee every photo listed by Google.

A download button appears in the bottom-right corner on any Google Maps page.
